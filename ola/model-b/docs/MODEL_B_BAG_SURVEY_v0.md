## Findings
1. Diameters fall into two groups. Standard bags are 13 to 14 in, and large bags are 16 to 19 in. No full-size cylindrical bag in the sample was under 13 in or over 19 in.
2. Weights run from 50 to 250 lb. The 70 to 100 lb range is heavily represented, and 150 lb and up are the large bags.
3. Water or "aqua" bags (Title Wave H2O 12 in, Rival Aqua 18 and 21 in) are rounded or unusually shaped, and their cover is a smooth water bladder. They're a poor fit for a friction collar.
4. Shaped bags (angle, uppercut, teardrop, wrecking ball, tapered "Big Bang") aren't cylinders, so a collar can't seat reliably on them.

## Recommended Model B compatibility rule (replaces v0 spec guesses)
- **Diameter:** 13 to 19 in, covered by **two collar sizes**: S/M for 13 to 15 in and L for 16 to 19 in. One band stretched across 13 to 19 in is possible, but two sizes give better grip. This is a design choice to confirm on paper.
- **Minimum weight:** 80 lb required, 100 lb or more recommended. Tilt at 80 lb is about 1.9 deg (spec section 4 estimate), and heavier bags swing less under arm reaction. The 80 lb floor is an ASSUMPTION pending the swing and reaction estimate.
- **Minimum length:** 42 in. The collar sits near the top third, which leaves a strike zone below it. Shorter bags (32 to 37 in) are excluded. ASSUMPTION pending the Blender layout.
- **Shape:** cylindrical only. Water, aqua and shaped bags are excluded at launch.
- **Hanging:** chain-hung with a top ring or swivel (unchanged). Freestanding comes later.
