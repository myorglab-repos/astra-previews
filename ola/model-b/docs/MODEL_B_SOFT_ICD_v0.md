# Model B: Soft Arm Interface Spec v0 (for Soft desk review)
Date: 2026-09-27 · Drafted by Ola for Ismael (Soft) review · Dimensions are ASSUMPTIONS. The soft arm itself carries over from the Model A Soft ICD, and only the mounting changes.

## What changes from Model A
| Item | Model A | Model B |
|---|---|---|
| Arm root | Rigid shoulder carrier on concealed support | Rotating ring on a clamp collar |
| Air supply | Remote compressor | Pack between the arm roots, valves under 30 cm from the arms |
| Reaction path | Into the frame | Collar, then bag and straps. Bag swings |
| Arm count | 2 | 2 (same) |

## Interface requirements
- SI-B1 Root flange: a quick-release bayonet on the ring. ASSUMPTION about 90 mm bolt circle, tool-free swap (wear part).
- SI-B2 Air: one supply line per chamber group, push-to-connect fittings. ASSUMPTION 6 mm OD tube, 4 lines per arm maximum.
- SI-B3 Pressure: same working band and burst margin as the Model A Soft ICD. The pack relief valve setting must be below arm burst divided by a 2x safety factor.
- SI-B4 Arm mass: 3 lb or less per arm (ASSUMPTION, from the mass budget).
- SI-B5 Contact force limit: 100 N or less per arm (this drives the collar grip estimate E1 and the swing estimate E3).
- SI-B6 Elastic return: the arm must return to its stowed position when vented, with no power.
- SI-B7 Nothing rigid exposed: the ring and flange sit behind the soft root boot.
