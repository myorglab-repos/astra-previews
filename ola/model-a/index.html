Model A: universal bag kit

Model A turns a punching bag you already own into a sparring partner. A slim padded shell straps onto the back of a hanging bag, and two soft air-powered arms, the same arms as Model B, reach around to the front. It is drawn to fit bags from 14 to 19 inches across. So far it exists only as a computer model: nothing has been built or tested.

01 — At a glance

  • Straps onto the back of a chain-hung punching bag the customer already owns.
  • Drawn to fit bags from 14 in to 19 in across (356 to 483 mm). Assumed, not tested.
  • A slim back shell about 78 mm (about 3 in) off the bag.
  • Arms identical to Model B's arms.
  • Narrow, smooth, plain black shoulder covers with no red. The gloves stay red.
  • Shoulders stick out 82 mm or less behind the shell.
  • Spend so far: $0. Digital only.

05 — What is under the padding

  • Soft air cells in each arm, held in knit sleeves, each with a short soft air feed. Eight cells per arm, the same as Model B.
  • Soft wrist cells and the glove.
  • Elastic return strips that pull each arm back after it moves.
  • At each shoulder: a rounded anchor eye and an eight-channel air manifold. This is simple placeholder hardware, not a finished shoulder.
  • On the back: thin curved mounting strips, two back webs, soft backing pads, and two bag straps with rear buckles.
  • Under the back shell: a battery and dock placeholder.
  • The bag and chains are the customer's, shown for context.

06 — Not in the model yet

The computer model now shows a full air and power pack, but it is not a working kit.The pack parts are assumed placeholder volumes, not selected or certified hardware. These items are still open:
  • Real hose routing, grommets and padding inside the pack are not designed yet. This work is in progress as v12. The closed-shell films hide the air lines and wiring inside the pack; that is a display choice, not a real re-route.
  • Left glove thumb: a known gap in the source glove model (SOURCE GAP). It has not been remodeled.
  • Strike impulse is not proven. These are Track A computer visualizations only. They do not prove air power, strike impulse, contact force, durability or camera tracking.
  • Critical items B-06, C-01, SAF-02 and HY-02 stay OPEN.
  • No parts chosen yet. No compressor, tank, valve, board or battery model has been selected. The relief valve, dump valve and battery latch are shown as intent only, with no ratings.
  • Nothing has been built, fitted to a real bag, or tested. Fit on any bag is assumed.

08 — What this does not prove

This lock is about shape and look only. It does not prove punch force, air power, contact force, durability, or camera tracking. Critical items C-01 and B-06 stay OPEN. Physical fit, moving clearance, material and foam build, how the kit stays on the bag, and room for the missing equipment are all still unproven. See-through (x-ray) views are a rendering trick, not real transparent parts.

09 — Design history v1 to v9

  • v1 compared three rear-mount ideas using the original arms; picked the rear-outrigger direction.
  • v2 restored Model B's materials and look; the 14 in bag showed a fit issue.
  • v3 rounded and smoothed the battery shell.
  • v4 merged the rear cover and shoulders into one shell (still 178.5 mm off the bag).
  • v5 introduced the 78 mm slim shell, segmented rear mount, smaller battery assumption; modeled both bag sizes.
  • v6 restored Model B's exact arm padding; rounded shoulder boots.
  • v7 tucked the shoulder covers in.
  • v8 made the shoulders rounder and smoother, with wider covers.
  • v9 narrowed each shoulder cover by about 36.7 mm to 298.15 mm. Locked by Michael, 6 Oct 2026, 12:39 pm ET.
