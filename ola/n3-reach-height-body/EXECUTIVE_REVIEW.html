Pass: n3-reach-height-body-atlas. The atlas and airflow amendment are complete for engineering review, with a mixed tall-reach verdict. Do not interpret completion of the packet as closure of RH-01 or a hardware gate.

Eight soft pneumatic continuum chambers per arm (U1-U3, F1-F3, T1-T2), textile strain limiting, soft wrist, vent plus elastic return and no exposed distal metal. The filled bag and mast stay fixed; the shoulder carrier alone has yaw. Shoulder pitch is a protected-root attitude concept; B-06 hardware remains unresolved.

Change required by the regression

The first candidate used 8/20/32-degree presets and inherited peak families. Tall high-diagonal motion penetrated the sampled proxy by 87.985 mm. It was rejected. The final policy uses 8/16/24-degree base presets, hook trim +3 degrees, high-diagonal trim -8 degrees and low-diagonal trim +15 degrees; body pitch is -20 degrees. An additional 60 mm sinusoidal outward U/F routing bow, consistently applied to every soft layer, return band and feed, preserves shoulder and wrist endpoints while clearing the sampled proxy. Total routing bow is 68 mm including the N3 baseline's 8 mm. This changes arc lengths and lateral envelope (RH-03); it is not a sewn pattern, pressure-equilibrium shape or assumed material capability.
