Six 50% cases are now labeled NON-HEAD-TARGET in the saved model and atlas. Their old overlapping target boxes are replaced with amber, separated diagnostic references; their reach limitations remain visible. Six 70% cases retain head-band surface inclusion and centroid-height inclusion. All 26 max cases retain their prior target results and all 38 geometric peak records retain their positions, palm frames and arc lengths.

Saved-model audit: 2431 integer/half-frame samples; G-03 minimum +1.792008 mm and G-05 minimum +4.999995 mm, exactly unchanged from accepted parent; A-05 maximum 0.000349603 mm. All current target/reference boxes clear the 275 mm infinite bag proxy. Nine ancestor hashes, including N1 and accepted AF-01b2, are preserved. The packet provides 38 labeled stills and three four-panel partial comparison sheets.

Eight soft continuum chambers per arm (U1-U3, F1-F3, T1-T2), textile strain limiting, vent plus elastic return, soft wrist and no exposed distal metal. Bag fill and mast remain fixed; only the shoulder carrier yaws. Protected-root pitch is prescribed, not qualified hardware travel.

Disposition requested: accept RH-02 as a scoped Track A policy closure using the explicit non-head-target alternative. Independent Ola acceptance is pending. 50% head-strike capability, physical loaded reach, narrow G-03 margin, RH-03, B-03 and Critical B-06/C-01 remain unresolved. AF-01b source acceptance is retained without a new swelling claim.

Track A does not prove strike impulse. No measured pneumatic propulsion, force, durability, loaded reach or real CV result is supplied.
