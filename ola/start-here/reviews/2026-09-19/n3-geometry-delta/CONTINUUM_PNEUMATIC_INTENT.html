U uses cyan-blue, F green, T magenta. Each branch has its own saved animated material and labeled bar for both arms. Distinct arbitrary display weights suggest common/differential emphasis; hue is channel identity, brightness is illustrative emphasis, amber marks vent. Inactive branches retain dim identity colors. The display is uncalibrated, not pressure, strain, flow, duty cycle or a valve command. T dominance is illustrative and does not establish physical twist sign. Mesh motion remains independently prescribed.

One schedule drives both phase labels and branch emphasis. In each active 240-frame chapter: 1-48 guard, 49-72 fill, 73-85 hold, 86-172 vent/elastic return, 173-240 guard. Inactive side stays guard. Vent releases drive; elastic textile is the return mechanism, not suction. Old generic N1 particle annotations are hidden in N2 to avoid a conflicting schedule.

Track B must identify chamber pressure-to-strain, bending/twist coupling, hysteresis, loaded tip response and vent/return behavior before any recipe is proposed.
