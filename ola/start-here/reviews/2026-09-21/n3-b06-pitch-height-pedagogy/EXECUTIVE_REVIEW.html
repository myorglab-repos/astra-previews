Remaining limits

The 8/16/24-degree base settings do not replace strike trims or partial-extension pitch policies. New covers are conceptual; B-06 load path, lock, pinch protection and service routing need engineering. The inherited narrow G-03 margin is unchanged, not a physical clearance rating.
