# Note

Boards were drawn in Python (Pillow) and encoded with ffmpeg, libx264, yuv420p, authored 12 fps. No Blender file was opened. The product blend was not saved.

`render_boards.py` rebuilds every card. Inherited RH-02 sheets are scaled copies of `reviews/2026-09-19/n3-rh02-partial-extension/media/partial_{short,mid,tall}.png`. Their original captions stay inside the picture. The new banner says they are prior policy stills.

12 fps is the film clock. It is not a measured camera, detector, or machine rate.

Raw frames under `raw/` are local rebuild scratch and are gitignored.
