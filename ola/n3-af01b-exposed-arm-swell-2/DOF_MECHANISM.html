E. Degrees of freedom and mechanism

Architecture remains eight soft chambers per arm (U1-U3, F1-F3, T1-T2), textile strain limiting, vent plus elastic return, and no exposed distal metal. Filled bag and mast remain fixed; the shoulder carrier alone provides yaw.

The inherited atlas prescribes shoulder carrier yaw, root pitch and continuum shapes; body strikes retain their inherited downward root pitch. Camera direction is a viewpoint, not bag rotation. The two exposed cameras are fixed within each chapter. Wrist T1/T2 geometry illustrates the soft wrist; cross/body palm-down, hook palm-inward, uppercut palm-up conventions are unchanged.

Track A does not prove strike impulse. Swelling, bend and timing are prescribed illustrations, not a pneumatic solution or measured pressure, flow, strain, propulsion, contact force, durability or real CV.
