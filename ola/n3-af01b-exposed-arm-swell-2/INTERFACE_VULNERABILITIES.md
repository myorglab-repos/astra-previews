# G. Interface vulnerabilities / DFMEA inputs

Design failure mode and effects analysis (DFMEA) inputs; no invented risk-priority scores.

| Named interface | Potential failure | Evidence still required |
|---|---|---|
| M1 L/R U/F chamber and integral soft feed objects | Seal rupture, kinking, local strain concentration | Coupon inflation and material/routing measurements; RH-03 |
| Elastic-return and retention-webbing objects | Slip, fatigue, asymmetric recoil | Physical retention and cycle tests; B-03 |
| T1/T2 to foam-textile glove | Retention loss, off-axis wrist deformation | Instrumented soft-wrist fixture |
| Recessed root, carrier, hub, bearing, mast and anchors | Load-path failure | Structural design and tests; Critical B-06 |
| Glove/contact interface | Unknown strike impulse and fault response | Instrumented Track C fixture; Critical C-01 |

**Track A does not prove strike impulse.** Swelling, bend and timing are prescribed illustrations, not a pneumatic solution or measured pressure, flow, strain, propulsion, contact force, durability or real CV.
