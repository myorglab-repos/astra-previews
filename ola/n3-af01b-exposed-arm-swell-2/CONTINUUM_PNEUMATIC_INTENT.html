D. Continuum pneumatic intent

Architecture remains eight soft chambers per arm (U1-U3, F1-F3, T1-T2), textile strain limiting, vent plus elastic return, and no exposed distal metal. Filled bag and mast remain fixed; the shoulder carrier alone provides yaw.

Eight separate manifold-to-cell feeds per arm. No U1-to-U2-to-U3 serial flow. Fill-more and fill-less are arbitrary display classes selected by the existing profile threshold of 0.65. All cells swell to some extent; all vent and return. No pressure or valve timing recipe is specified. Cyan dots/branch arrows move toward cells during fill; orange cues reverse during vent; gold textile marks passive recoil while venting.
