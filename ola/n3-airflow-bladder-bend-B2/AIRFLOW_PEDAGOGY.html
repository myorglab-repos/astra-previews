Read the exposed arm together with the lower branch panel. Every cell is labeled; the stronger-fill group is named beneath the view. Cyan dots travel toward cells along their own modeled feed, and animated cyan arrows move from individual manifold ports toward separate cell icons. Orange dots and arrows reverse toward the manifold/exhaust during vent. Gold textile bands mark passive return. Root hardware is hidden in the exposed pane to reduce occlusion; its absence is not a completed hardware design.

All eight active branches receive illustrative fill during FILL/STEER, with more/less emphasis. This intentionally replaces Amendment A's low-emphasis vent labels: low branches now swell less, so color and shape tell the same story. Hold preserves the larger cell shapes. Vent and return reduce volume as the inherited section shapes return to guard. No serial U1-to-U2 feed is depicted.

The cell meshes use per-ring radial scaling: guard scale 0.45; peak scale 0.45 + 1.35 × arbitrary branch emphasis squared. These are dimensionless drawing choices, not measured strain. Ring centers retain the inherited bend; swelling and bending share the same interpolation weight. The body chapter also includes inherited downward root pitch. T-cell emphasis accompanies the prescribed palm frame; it is not a verified torsional response.

This teaches a proposed differential inflation/strain-limiting mechanism: unequal chamber expansion can bias a textile-constrained soft section, while the actual direction and magnitude require System ID. Swelling is scripted alongside bend, not the output of a pneumatic equilibrium solve. Do not infer from the illustration that a particular numbered physical cell has a proven bending sign.
