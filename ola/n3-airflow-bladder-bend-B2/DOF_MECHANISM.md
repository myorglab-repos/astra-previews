# E. Degrees of freedom and mechanism

The filled bag and mast are stationary. Shoulder-carrier yaw remains the only product rotation about the mast; changing a camera view is not rotating bag fill. Protected-root pitch and prescribed continuum shape keys are inherited. The straight/hook/uppercut/body palm frames remain down/inward/up/down.

The new cell radial morph is a drawing parameter, not an added physical joint or a newly identified degree of freedom. Eight chambers, textile strain limiting, soft torsion wrist and vent plus elastic return remain the plant concept. B-06 protected-root mechanics remain incomplete.

**Track A does not prove strike impulse.** These are prescribed geometry and illustrative pneumatic cues. Measured propulsion, contact force, durability and real CV remain unverified.
