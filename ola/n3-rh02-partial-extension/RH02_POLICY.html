RH-02 partial-extension aim policy

The brief expressly allows a partial stroke to be declared non-head-target. Apply that option to short/mid/tall × left/right at 50%: short_short_L_straight, short_short_R_straight, mid_short_L_straight, mid_short_R_straight, tall_short_L_straight, tall_short_R_straight. These six chapters are NON-HEAD-TARGET rehearsals, not head shots or newly designated body shots. No alternate body target or contact capability is claimed.

Their amber box is a reference only, Y=-0.600..-0.300 m, used to report the miss at the same stand-off as 70%. The original 50% box (Y=-0.450..-0.150 m) overlapped the 275 mm bag proxy by 125 mm and is rejected as a current target. It is retained in rh02_prior_target and the immutable parent evidence. The new reference has 25 mm positive radial separation. X=-0.260..+0.260 m and the original Z bands stay unchanged: short 1.450..1.650 m, mid 1.620..1.820 m, tall 1.780..1.960 m.

All six 70% straight chapters retain HEAD TARGET at Y=-0.600..-0.300 m. Acceptance is glove surface vertices inside the target at peak plus glove centroid height inside the declared head band. It is not full-glove containment, centered XY aim, or a claim that a human can stand safely at that spacing. Tall 70% centroid lies 6.305 mm behind the near Y face even though surface inclusion and centroid height pass; report this rather than moving the box.

The inherited coupled pitch/shape policy remains: short/mid/tall 50% pitches 27.126/49.181/57.000 degrees, 70% pitches 13.499/29.110/45.018 degrees; existing shape weights and time schedules are unchanged. The 57-degree cap is a review constraint, not a hardware rating. 50/70% names denote inherited approximate shoulder-to-wrist chord fractions, not measured pneumatic stroke. No mesh/chord lengthening or additional routing bow. No max case is superseded.

Use N3_REACH_HEIGHT_BODY_ATLAS in the successor for this policy. Other inherited educational scenes retain their historical scope and are not RH-02 policy evidence. Different chapters use different opponent assumptions; this is not a single simultaneous opponent placement. Rehearsal chapters are discrete cuts, not certified transitions.

RH-02 is a Track A policy closure candidate for Ola. The excluded 50% head capability remains unavailable; this pass does not solve it mechanically. Track A does not prove strike impulse. No measured pneumatic propulsion, force, durability, loaded reach or real CV result is supplied.
